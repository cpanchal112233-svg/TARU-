# TARU Vision

## Current product

TARU is a **personal health organizer and companion** for Android and iOS.

Today TARU helps people:

- keep self-reported health information together (profile, conditions, allergies, medicines)
- stay on top of medicine and lifestyle routines
- record daily activity and review Progress factually
- record weight and blood pressure history
- store medical reports and extract text on-device for personal review
- export a TARU archive of stored information
- delete health data or delete the account
- optionally share technical crash diagnostics (off by default)
- use bounded, rule-based caution and urgency tools where already shipped

TARU does **not** currently diagnose conditions, interpret reports clinically,
recommend treatments, act as a medical device, or provide an AI health
assistant.

## Mission

Help people live healthier lives by organizing their own health information,
supporting daily routines, and preparing carefully for future grounded
assistance — always in partnership with healthcare providers.

## Long-term vision

Become a trusted personal health companion by combining a durable health
record, daily action tools, longitudinal learning, and — when safety,
consent, and evidence design are ready — grounded assistance that cites
the user’s own data rather than inventing clinical conclusions.

Future directions may include grounded AI assistance, platform health
integrations (for example HealthKit / Health Connect), broader
accessibility and localization, and stronger reliability. Those remain
**future work**, not present capabilities.
