# TARU Vision

TARU is an AI-powered personal health companion that helps people understand their health, build healthy habits, organize medical information, and make informed decisions in partnership with their healthcare providers.

## Mission

Help millions of people live healthier lives through personalized guidance, education, and technology.

## Long-Term Vision

Become the world's most trusted AI-powered health companion by combining modern medical knowledge, healthy lifestyle coaching, and user-friendly technology.
